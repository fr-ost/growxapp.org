## 2. Broken-link building: BlackMagic and Hypefury (do this first)

**Background.** BlackMagic, the X analytics tool owned by Hypefury, shut down on 2026-07-01, and Hypefury's own site says it no
longer supports X. Many "best Twitter analytics tools" and "best Twitter tools" articles still link to them, so their readers land on
a dead page or a tool that no longer serves X. Telling the author is a genuine favour, and it is a natural moment to suggest a
replacement.

Before you start, open blackmagic.so and hypefury.com yourself and confirm what they say today. Never tell an author a tool is dead
unless you have checked it on the day you write.

### Step by step

1. **Find pages that link to them.** Search Google and Bing with operators such as:
   - `"blackmagic.so" twitter analytics`
   - `"blackmagic.so" -site:blackmagic.so`
   - `"BlackMagic" "Twitter analytics" tools`
   - `intitle:"twitter analytics tools" blackmagic`
   - `"blackmagic.so" "best twitter tools"`
   - `"hypefury.com" "twitter scheduler" -site:hypefury.com`
   - `"BlackMagic" "Hypefury" twitter`

   If you have access to a backlink checker, a report of pages linking to blackmagic.so gives a fuller list than search alone.
2. **Qualify each page.** Keep it only if it is a real article (not a scraped copy), neutral (not a competitor's own blog), written
   for X creators or marketers, and has a way to contact the author or site. Pages updated in the last couple of years are the most
   likely to be updated again.
3. **Check the link.** Click it in a private window and note exactly what happens: an error, a timeout, a redirect, or a shutdown
   notice. Write down the section heading and the sentence the link sits in. If the link still works and the tool's site says it is
   running, skip the page.
4. **Pick the replacement that fits the sentence**, not the one we like best:
   - A list of analytics tools: https://www.growxapp.org/blog/twitter-analytics-tools (the neutral guide) or
     https://www.growxapp.org/features/twitter-analytics
   - Follower or unfollower tracking: https://www.growxapp.org/features/twitter-unfollower-tracker
   - Scheduling, evergreen posts or auto-plugs (the Hypefury side): https://www.growxapp.org/alternatives/hypefury
5. **Find the right contact**: the author's page, the site's contact form or the address the site publishes for corrections. Don't
   guess addresses and don't store them in this repo.
6. **Send the email below.** One polite follow-up after about a week, then stop.
7. **Log it** in the tracker (section 6).

## 3. Unlinked brand mentions

People sometimes mention GrowX in a roundup, a forum post or a video description without linking to it. Asking for a link there is
low effort and usually welcome.

1. **Search** for mentions without a link to the site:
   - `"GrowX" twitter -site:growxapp.org`
   - `"GrowX" "Chrome extension" -site:growxapp.org`
   - `"growxapp" -site:growxapp.org`
   - `"GrowX" "auto follow"` and `"GrowX" "X growth"`
2. **Set up alerts** for "GrowX" with Twitter or X, and for "growxapp", so new mentions reach you without searching.
3. **Make sure it is us.** Other businesses use the name GrowX. Contact a site only when the mention is clearly about the Chrome
   extension for X (Twitter).
4. **Check what they wrote.** If a detail is wrong (a price, a feature, the free plan), the correction matters more than the link.
   Use `PRODUCT_FACTS.md` and the live pricing page.
5. **Send the email below**, once. If the mention is in a forum or comment thread, reply in the thread instead of emailing, and only
   if a link genuinely helps the reader.

> Subject: Thanks for mentioning GrowX
>
> Hi {name},
>
> Thanks for including GrowX in "{title}". I build it, and it was nice to come across.
>
> If you're happy to, a link to https://www.growxapp.org would help readers find the right extension, since a few other products
> share the name. {If something is out of date: One small update: {correction}.}
>
> Screenshots and a short description are at https://www.growxapp.org/press if you ever need them.

> Shahriar

## 4. Podcast and YouTube guest pitch

Creator-economy, indie-hacker and social-media-marketing shows regularly host tool builders. A good episode brings a link in the
show notes and listeners who already care about growing on X.

**Who to pitch.** Small and mid-sized shows that have had founders or tool builders as guests in their last ten episodes, YouTube
channels that review creator tools, and indie developer shows. Listen to at least one episode first and mention it.

**Topic angles** (pick one per show, the one closest to their last few episodes):

- **Growing on X without spam**: pacing, follow-back scoring, why a tool should slow down after any warning from X, and why no tool
  can promise zero risk.
- **Platform risk for creators**: what to do when the tools you rely on leave X or shut down, as Hypefury and BlackMagic did in 2026.
- **AI in the browser**: writing help with Chrome's built-in model on the user's own computer, without sending drafts to a server.
- **Pricing a solo-built tool**: a free plan forever, short prepaid Premium periods with no auto-renew, and what that means for users.
- **Going multilingual as a solo developer**: translating a Chrome extension into 21 languages.

**What to bring.** A demo with demo data, the honest limits (posts and auto follow need Chrome open; desktop only), and the facts from
`PRODUCT_FACTS.md`. No follower promises, no invented numbers, no user counts.

**Paid slots.** If a show charges for a guest spot, it is an ad: it must be disclosed as sponsored and any link marked
`rel="sponsored"`. When in doubt, skip it.

> Subject: Guest idea for {show}: {angle title}
>
> Hi {name},
>
> I enjoyed your episode on {episode topic}, especially {specific point}.
>
> I build GrowX, a Chrome extension for growing on X (Twitter), and I'd like to suggest an episode on {angle title}. A few things I
> could cover:
>
> - {point 1}
> - {point 2}
> - {point 3}
>
> I'll keep it practical for your listeners rather than a product pitch, and I'm happy to be clear about what tools like mine can't
> do. More about me and the project: https://www.growxapp.org/press
>
> Thanks for considering it,
> Shahriar

After the episode: share it, thank the host, and ask once whether the show notes can link to the page that matches the topic (for
example https://www.growxapp.org/blog/twitter-growth-plan for a growth episode).

## 5. Rules reminder

- No paid links unless the site marks them `rel="sponsored"`; no link farms, private blog networks or link exchanges at scale.
- No fake or incentivised reviews, and never ask people to review without using the extension.
- Disclose that you build GrowX in every email, answer and episode.
- Never promise follower counts or results, and never say GrowX is risk-free: "No tool can promise zero risk on X."
- Describe other products only by what they say publicly, and check their current status before saying a tool has closed.
- Keep contact details out of this repo; they live in your mail client.
