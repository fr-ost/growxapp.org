## 5. Guest post pitches (outlines; write them in your own words)

**A. For developer communities (DEV, Hashnode, Indie Hackers):** "What I learned translating a Chrome extension into 21 languages"
- Why: the 2.4.7 work is a genuine engineering story, and these sites allow a profile link and a canonical link back.
- Outline: picking languages from your audience; one dictionary file per language; translating notifications from the service worker;
  right-to-left layout for Arabic; never translating user text; testing with long German and short Chinese strings.
- Link once to https://www.growxapp.org/whats-new, in context.

**B. For marketing and creator blogs:** "X's daily follow limit: what actually happens and how to work with it"
- Outline: what the daily limit looks like from the user's side; why bursts are risky; pacing with delays and breaks; why a tool should
  wait just under the number X allowed instead of retrying (what GrowX 2.4.6 does); checking the 5,000 following cap with the
  follower ratio calculator.
- Link to https://www.growxapp.org/tools/twitter-follower-ratio-calculator or https://www.growxapp.org/blog/mass-follow-twitter-limits.

Pitch email: reuse the one in `guest-posts.md` and swap in the title and outline.

## 6. Reviews on the Chrome Web Store

More honest reviews help both rankings and conversions. Since 2.4.5 the extension asks once for a review after a real win, and it is
easy to skip. Don't add incentives, don't ask friends to review without using it, and reply politely to every review, good or bad.
